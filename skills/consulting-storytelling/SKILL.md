# Storytelling v2
For complete slides use consulting-slide-design. For standalone work follow the supplied Universal Consulting Slide Prompt v2 where available. Establish the audience and decision, one source-backed conclusion, English action title <=20 words and <=2 lines, maximum three MECE blocks, and a decision- or number-led so-what. Map every block to a meaningful chart or diagram, with one dominant hero exhibit and distinct supporting visuals. Keep running copy around 100 words total and captions under 20 words with bold leads. Preserve evidence and caveats; do not invent data to satisfy visual targets. Deliver the title, evidence chain, visual plan and caveats. Rationale in Vietnamese. No rendered slide or QA claim for argument-only work.
