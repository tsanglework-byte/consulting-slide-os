# Final quality gate (visual-first v2)
Record Pass / Fail / Not checked, evidence and corrective action per item. Never equate missing tools with a pass.
- Exactly one 16:9 slide; English visible text; one defensible message; action title <=20 words and <=2 lines; at most three MECE blocks; approximately <=100 running words (excluding labels/notes/sources); captions start with bold lead and <20 words; quantified or decision-led so-what.
- Every block visually anchored; >=3 visuals across >=2 types; hero chart >=1/3 of body area (measure where feasible); chart density >=5 data points OR >=3 series; >=1 think-cell-style annotation per chart; focus item, units, period, direct labels; 3–4 key figures if supported; prototype for solution/product/process when applicable.
- Original layout/palette: 0.5-inch margins, three-column grid, badge, logo placeholder, runner, header pills and legible typography (title 24–28 pt, headers 12–14 pt, body and chart labels >=10 pt, all text >=9 pt), consistent brand, no clutter, clipping or unintended overlaps. Verify rendered dimensions and actual text sizes when possible.
- All numbers sourced or calculated only from supplied values; assumptions/qualifications visible, source superscripts match notes; no invented values, no false template paths; Appendix Note formula/input/source and Data to confirm.
- Correct mode; PPTX native charts/shapes where feasible and editability checked separately from think-cell; IMAGE high-resolution preview with label/data inspection; BLUEPRINT exact copy, layout choice and 40–60-word speaker notes. Every mode includes Build Sheet and Quality Check; no extra slide.
If rules are infeasible because of evidence scarcity or tool limits, disclose the failure or unverified status and deliver the best truthful result. Do not pad data or claim guaranteed partner approval/readability in ten seconds without testing.
