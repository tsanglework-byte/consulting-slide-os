# Exhibit selection (v2 companion)
The original prompt controls composition. Chart data must come from the supplied inputs. Use a period, unit, source, direct labels and at least one meaningful think-cell-style annotation.
- Time: line or column; CAGR only with positive comparable endpoints and known periods. Formula (end/start)^(1/n)-1.
- Bridge: build-up/down waterfall; reconcile opening + signed drivers = ending.
- Composition: stacked/100% bar, doughnut (max five parts) or Mekko with meaningful area/width and reconciled totals.
- Comparison: clustered bars and difference arrow; combination chart for rate if axes are unambiguous.
- Relationships: scatter/bubble with focus item and explicit units; correlation is not causation.
- Scenarios: line or stacked area with labelled assumptions; schedule: Gantt or timeline with milestones.
- Qualitative: matrix, pyramid, Venn, funnel, flow or prototype; don't invent numbers to force a chart.
For Library objects, use the user-supplied categories as candidate search terms, not verified current folder paths. Check installed Library or provided screenshots; record exact verified name or mark unverified and specify a shape-based substitute. Official documentation: https://www.think-cell.com/en/resources/manual/library and https://www.think-cell.com/en/resources/manual/slide-templates.
For insufficient data (fewer than five points and fewer than three series), retain actual values and mark the density rule unmet instead of creating fake observations.
