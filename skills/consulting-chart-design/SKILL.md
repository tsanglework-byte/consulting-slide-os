# Chart design v2
For complete slides use consulting-slide-design. Identify the analytical relationship, units, periods, sources and assumptions; select an appropriate chart (bar, line, waterfall, Mekko, combination, stacked, scatter, bubble, timeline or other suitable type). Use direct labels, one highlighted focus item, honest scales, and at least one meaningful annotation (CAGR, difference arrow, value line, bracket, total, connector or call-out). Aim for five points or three series per chart as required by v2; if data is insufficient, preserve facts and flag the exception. Never invent values or sources. Deliver datasheet categories in rows and series in columns, unit in headers, colours, annotations, calculated metrics and formulas. Name only verified Library templates; otherwise label candidates unverified. Do not claim native think-cell objects without verified tools.
